# START HERE — Yellow Beast Alpha

Launch **Yellow Beast.command** on macOS or **Yellow Beast.bat** on Windows. Choose **Async: Clear-Q4 — PLAYABLE ALPHA**.

Clear-Q4 is a complete operation: create or resume a world, confirm your generated team, review Standard’s briefing, configure issued equipment and containers, enter the Complex, investigate branching routes, manage check-ins and delayed radio contact, direct coworkers, respond to hazards, collect and containerize evidence, return or abort, reconcile accountability, and receive a state-derived debrief and follow-up.

Use the visible controls or natural language such as `Inspect the fixture`, `Move to the relay alcove`, `Test the panel`, `Report the evidence`, or `Return`. The inventory surface provides both compact contextual actions and a complete keyboard-accessible action list. Unavailable actions explain the grounded requirement. The bundled Offline Interpreter selects only authored legal actions and works without an API key.

OpenAI presentation is optional. Set `OPENAI_API_KEY`, then `YELLOW_BEAST_AI_PROVIDER=openai` (and optionally `YELLOW_BEAST_AI_MODEL=gpt-5.6-luna`) in your operating-system environment. Structured simulation resolves first; AI wording cannot change mission, hazard, inventory, team, route, communication, or institutional truth. The deterministic fallback always remains available.

Saves, configuration, and launcher logs live outside the app:

- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Yellow Beast/`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Yellow Beast\`

Relaunch and accept **Resume saved Async: Clear-Q4 run?** to continue the exact record. Current alpha limits: only Async: Clear-Q4 is a complete authored operation; other modes are experimental; team and Standard autonomy are intentionally bounded; and final pacing, accessibility certification, audiovisual polish, performance profiling, installer polish, and external beta remain for Pass 10. If startup fails, inspect `logs/launcher.log` in the application-data directory.
