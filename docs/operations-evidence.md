# Expedition communication and evidence boundaries

Yellow Beast keeps an objective event, sender intent, transmitted message, recipient access, and later interpretation separate. A sent message is not universally received; an institutional document is not objective truth merely because it appears official.

## Directly checked operational evidence

YB-4 adds *Backrooms - Motion Detected* as a directly checked official source. Its on-screen card establishes only a location-bound camera arrangement, stated alert behavior, and stated recording behavior. It does not name an alert recipient, define radio behavior, prove a universal evidence chain, or establish a general ASYNC doctrine.

`operations/communication-records.json` maps the existing Threshold Baseline delivered event to an explicit actual-recipient set. That private radio representation remains `pack_original`; it demonstrates Custodian's delivery boundary rather than a claim about universal ASYNC equipment.

## Evidence objects and custody

`operations/evidence-objects.json` is Yellow Beast declarative provenance metadata. Its `creation_ref` points to the public world-pack execution rule that creates the existing Custodian evidence event. Custody names, transfer records, claim links, integrity, and observation limits are validated by Yellow Beast but do not mutate Custodian or replace canonical replay provenance.

The baseline measurement is intentionally simple: alpha creates and retains it, and export/restore is checked to preserve the objective Custodian evidence array. No general archival procedure, forensic score, or cryptographic authenticity mechanism is claimed.

## Raw record versus interpretation

The camera statement in *Motion Detected* supports a bounded raw-record claim. The identity of any alert recipient remains `needs-context`. The existing institutional-assertion claim remains reference-only. Later reports may support, qualify, or contradict records through typed claim relationships; they may never overwrite the raw event or observer-local access by declaration.

## Reviewer checklist

1. Directly check the official locator and timestamp.
2. State only visible or explicitly stated behavior.
3. Record recipient access independently from message emission.
4. Give every evidence object a valid creator, origin reference, custodian, and linked claim.
5. Keep absent recipients absent—do not infer universal delivery.
6. Mark simulation-only radio, custody, and action details `pack-original`.
7. Reject location-bound observations as proof of universal doctrine.
