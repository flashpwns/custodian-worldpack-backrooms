## 1. Executive baseline

The audited repository is a functioning, deterministic, action-coupled Clear-Q4 vertical slice with unusually strong canonical-state, observer-boundary, provenance, and packaged-profile isolation foundations. It is **not** an implementation of the current Gameplay Constitution as a whole.

The current player loop is a native Electron record library, name-only personnel creation, a collapsed preparation/deployment flow, a bounded field-operation interface, discrete player ACTION resolution, LOCAL/STANDARD communication, persistent generated geography, assignments, equipment custody, evidence records, debrief, and explicit between-operation advancement. Most meaningful world change occurs only when a player action or explicit operations-advance command calls the operational cycle. There is no continuous simulation clock, 1991 calendar, pause/idle autonomy, substantial offline progression, navigable ASYNC facility, full personnel/relationship/career system, complete onboarding, real audio system, or ordinary production path that reliably activates the implemented Still Life/Bacteria encounter machinery.

The implementation baseline is therefore:

> **A credible action-driven field-operations prototype with several preservation-grade authorities, but not yet the living workplace simulation promised by the current vision.**

The most consequential findings are:

1. **The living-turn model is missing.** `operational-time`, team decisions, environmental updates, communications, and phenomenon advancement run inside discrete operational cycles initiated by player/runtime calls. The world does not advance continuously between ACTION submissions.
2. **The current vision conflicts with the highest authority in two places.** Same-world post-death careers conflict with Simulation Doctrine §19.14–19.17. Broad real-time/offline consequences lack the explicit narrow override required by Doctrine §2.9 and §21.11. These conflicts require human ratification; implementation must not guess.
3. **Persistence has a real unresolved defect.** The long-world torture report is deterministic but reports `save_reload_equivalent: false` and `passed: false`. The official `npm test` command omits that test.
4. **The first-five-minutes promise is largely absent.** Only first/last name entry exists. The letter, liability text, projector/canonical film, full personnel attributes, hidden work-history interpretation, coworker files, equipment request/approval, Threshold approach, and staged reveal are missing. The current UI deliberately collapses briefing through Threshold into one preparation surface and one `DEPLOY` action.
5. **Personnel are persistent records, not yet full people.** Deterministic tendencies, qualifications, shared operational history, custody, salience, and bounded reactions exist. Relationships, fears, preferences, emotional state, career motives, promotion/discipline/retirement, and genuine expedition leadership do not.
6. **Geography, evidence, communication, assignments, and observer safety are the strongest gameplay foundations.** They should be retained and extended, not rewritten wholesale.
7. **Threat behavior is code-complete only in controlled/scaffolded form.** Production eligibility can instantiate phenomena, but the player-accessible initiating stimuli and Bacteria acquisition/mimic/slam controls are predominantly developer-fixture operations. The repository's own Still Life source authority also prohibits the behaviors the current Constitution requires.
8. **Packaging viability is currently good at this HEAD.** Build, packaged smoke, packaged renderer interaction, native Settings interaction, and native first-run create/reopen verification all passed. The known malformed non-ASCII root-directory anomaly did not reproduce.
9. **Historical completion claims are not reliable evidence of current vision completion.** `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATE.md` and old pass summaries accurately describe many implemented components, but nearly all historical human gates remain pending and the old campaign targeted a materially narrower product.

No new pass sequence is proposed here. Section 13 gives dependency relationships only, as required for later reconciliation.

## 5. Existing systems worth preserving

These systems are substantively aligned with the Constitution and Doctrine and should remain authoritative during future work:

1. **World history and canonical authority separation** — persistent identities/events, explicit owning authorities, immutable retired-world enforcement until/unless doctrine is amended.
2. **Observer-safe projection** — personnel contact, survey frontier, scene facts, evidence access, and provider-safe context are separated from canonical state.
3. **Provider/interpreter contract** — candidate-only generation, bounded context, validation, deterministic fallback, and no provider canonical writes.
4. **STANDARD/communication state machine** — attempted transmission is distinct from delivery/acknowledgment and institutional knowledge requires provenance.
5. **Institutional cognition** — confirmed knowledge, uncertain claims, pending decisions, and follow-up work are persistent and causal.
6. **Persistent procedural geography** — deterministic expansion, canonical snapshots, blocked/transient connection support, separate observer maps, and save restoration.
7. **Evidence authority** — provenance, custody, reporting, institutional availability, contradictions, and presentation-only rendering.
8. **Assignment-condition engine** — world/institution/evidence/geography conditions can generate work through bounded templates.
9. **Equipment/logistics authority** — item identity, custody, containers, handoff, loss/damage/service, and spatial synchronization.
10. **NPC task foundation** — requests create persistent tasks with refusal/delay/unheard states and observer-bounded contact.
11. **Outcome/accountability records** — missing/dead distinction, final incident, personnel/equipment/evidence reconciliation.
12. **Atomic save/recovery design** — current/previous-good validation, transactional session writes, damaged-save preservation, and profile isolation, subject to the long-world defect.
13. **Packaged test-profile isolation** — the current recovery work prevents native verification from mutating the production user profile.
14. **Mundane restraint** — sparse eligibility, caps/cooldowns, salience-based silence, and avoidance of arbitrary offscreen death.

## 6. Systems requiring repair

1. **Long-world persistence equivalence** — highest-priority executable defect. The deterministic report cannot pass while save/reload diverges.
2. **Test aggregation and exit semantics** — all authoritative tests must be intentionally included or explicitly categorized; failed JSON reports must return a failing process status or be wrapped by a failing assertion.
3. **LOCAL whitespace fallback** — an all-silent response must render meaningful silence, not whitespace.
4. **Renderer refresh guard** — `requestContext().worldId === context.mode` is an invalid comparison and prevents valid refresh application (`desktop/renderer/renderer.js:190`).
5. **Settings completeness** — expose and persist the already-supported audio, reduced-sensory, reopen, small-text, and light-theme controls or deliberately remove unsupported backend settings after authority review.
6. **Canon closure claims** — `runtime_authority_complete: true` should not be treated as substantive canon completion while most sources are deferred/partial and Still Life behavior is explicitly unadmitted.

8. **Human acceptance debt** — UI, interaction, accessibility, audio, long-play, and external stranger gates remain unverified despite automated pass summaries.

## 11. Architectural conflicts with the Gameplay Constitution

### 11.1 Same-world successor careers conflict with Simulation Doctrine

The Gameplay Constitution offers “begin a new career immediately in the same world” and permits a pre-expedition reload (`Gameplay Constitution:645-667`). The vision roadmap says player death “does not necessarily end the world” (`Vision-Centric Roadmap:606-612`).

Simulation Doctrine is unambiguous:

- death is irreversible and ordinary load may not rewind it (`SIMULATION_DOCTRINE.md:13642-13653`);
- if controlled personnel dies, the active career ends, the world becomes historical, and the player may create another world (`13657-13667`);
- the world is retired and immutable (`13671-13713`).

Neither new vision document explicitly declares a Doctrine amendment or superseding rule. The current implementation correctly follows the Doctrine, while its dormant `selectSuccessor` function points toward the conflicting roadmap. This is not an implementation-choice problem. It is an authority problem requiring human amendment or vision correction.

### 11.2 Broad real-time/offline consequences lack the required Doctrine override

The Gameplay Constitution requires outside days to follow real elapsed time and allows substantial offline institutional/personnel consequences, including missing coworkers (`Gameplay Constitution:264-293,612-637`).

Doctrine says application closure does not by default equal elapsed-world simulation; a worldpack may authorize it only deliberately with bounded deterministic rules (`SIMULATION_DOCTRINE.md:1366-1380`). Doctrine further says offscreen activity is bounded and may not casually kill important personnel or discover/resolve major phenomena merely because the player was absent (`1747-1773`). Doctrine §21.11 requires explicit override language plus defined scope, consequences, persistence, and observer impact (`15773-15790`).

The current vision states the desired behavior but does not frame it as a narrow Doctrine override or define adequate causal limits. The existing bounded shift processor follows the older authority. Implementing broad offline outcomes now would silently weaken observer/canonical integrity.

### 11.3 Save/reload convenience conflicts with irreversible canonical death unless checkpoints are explicitly metagame state

The Constitution permits reloading a pre-expedition save outside simulation canon while also saying there are no mid-expedition saves. Doctrine forbids ordinary load from undoing canonical death. The runtime currently autosaves every accepted field action and transaction, then atomically retires on death. A future checkpoint system must explicitly separate canonical world history from metagame attempt/reset state; otherwise reload becomes a canonical rewrite.

### 11.4 Still Life vision conflicts with repository canon authority

The Constitution and Simulation Doctrine describe broad heterogeneous Still Life behavior. `data/still-life-behavior-authority.json` says the current corpus contains no admitted Still Life behavior source and explicitly prohibits generalizing appearance, movement, perception, pursuit, harm, or motives. Code nevertheless implements eight behavior profiles. Until sources are admitted or the authority is amended, production use would be canonically unsupported.

### 11.5 Current runtime conflicts with continuous-world and leadership promises

These are implementation conflicts rather than document-authority conflicts:

- operational time and all autonomous subsystems advance only on discrete calls;
- the player is assigned practical field authority every expedition;
- accepted actions autosave during the Complex;
- the facility is a preparation surface rather than navigable institutional geography.

They must be corrected at their owning runtime/design authorities, not hidden through renderer copy.

## 12. Highest-risk unknowns

1. **What is the ratified death/world rule?** Same-world successor careers and Doctrine-mandated world retirement cannot both be canonical.
2. **What exact offline override is authorized?** Scope, maximum consequences, deterministic budget, replay model, observer knowledge, and protection against arbitrary personnel loss are undefined.
3. **What causes long-world save/reload divergence?** Until isolated, every new persistent subsystem increases corruption risk.
4. **Are Still Life/Bacteria behaviors supported by admitted primary canon?** The repository currently cannot prove this for Still Life and verifies only two primary sources overall.
5. **What player-visible work is actually desired from the three locked historical modes?** They materially enlarge maintenance/package scope but are outside the current Clear-Q4 vision campaign.
6. **How will continuous time remain deterministic?** Scheduler cadence, resolution ordering, event queues, pause, process suspension, catch-up, and crash recovery are unspecified.
7. **How will no-mid-expedition-save coexist with desktop crash recovery?** A strict absence of persistence risks losing canonical consequences; invisible recovery checkpoints risk violating the player promise unless defined carefully.
8. **What is the admissible interpretation contract for player work history?** It must create candidate competencies without provider authority, hidden-state invention, bias, or player-visible RPG statistics.
9. **Can the current spatial model support long-lived contradiction without parallel truth authorities?** Transient edges, observer maps, and canonical snapshots exist, but ordinary mutation/reconciliation has not been stress-tested.
10. **Are the 31 audio source files licensed/admitted for distribution?** The audio authority requests a rights check, and no runtime assets currently ship.
11. **Does the UI meet human comprehension/accessibility expectations?** Automated selector tests cannot establish layout, text density, screen-reader flow, visual hierarchy, or horror timing.
12. **Will the known malformed-directory anomaly recur under a wider Electron matrix?** It did not reproduce here, but prior operator evidence remains an open harness finding.
13. **What is the intended ancestry expectation beyond the current HEAD?** This audit verified exact continuity with the prior full-suite audit, but no external target commit/remote ancestry requirement was supplied.

## 13. Recommended dependency order for future implementation

This is a dependency graph, not a proposed pass sequence.

1. **Ratify authority conflicts first.** Resolve death/world retirement, same-world careers, offline real-time progression, save/reload metagame semantics, and Still Life canon authority. No implementation should pre-empt these decisions.
2. **Repair evidence trust.** Fix long-world persistence, make the authoritative test aggregate comprehensive, and correct report exit semantics. Establish reliable baseline gates before adding persistent state.
3. **Define the canonical time/scheduler contract.** Specify time domains, event ordering, pause, idle, process suspension, catch-up, deterministic randomness, and persistence boundaries.
4. **Build continuous simulation on existing domain authorities.** Schedule team intents, communications, environment, institution, and phenomena without moving truth into renderer/provider code.
5. **Expand the personnel/authority model.** Hidden competencies, memory, relationships, emotion, career state, leadership, and staffing must exist before dialogue, evaluation, and equipment approval can be truthful.
6. **Define and implement career/institution loops.** Evaluations, standing, privileges, assignments, equipment approval, personnel availability, and facility state should consume the personnel/institution authorities.
7. **Build the facility/onboarding cockpit.** Only after the underlying personnel/equipment/career state can drive it; otherwise it becomes decorative menus.
8. **Extend geography/evidence/phenomena after canon admission.** Use the existing spatial, survey, evidence, and observer systems; add production causes and contradiction without parallel authorities.
9. **Implement ratified persistence history/offline/multi-career behavior.** Stress it with deterministic long-world and cross-career tests before presentation polish.
10. **Integrate causal audio and final presentation.** Audio must consume canonical/observer-safe events, ship admitted assets, obey settings, and never become a truth source.
11. **Run native human and stranger gates.** First-day comprehension, room test, relationship recognition, facility internship, long absence, couch/phenomenon encounter, death, accessibility, audio, packaging, and external cold start.
