# Project Reference Expedition — Invariants

**Status:** Milestone contract, not a claim of current completion. **Purpose:** prove one trustworthy, replayable expedition before wider reconstruction. **Authority:** Simulation Doctrine → admitted canon/source policy → current Yellow Beast product authorities → this specimen constraint.

**Thesis.** A player-led, four-person A-Sync team performs a routine layout/survey assignment through established infrastructure into a modest frontier section containing one subtle structural discrepancy of unresolved cause and meaning.

1. **Work before horror.** Briefing, accountability, equipment, team presence, radio, Threshold crossing, route-following, survey, documentation, and return are the experience. Quiet and an otherwise successful survey are valid. *(Basis: Gameplay Constitution §§1–3; Reconstruction Authority §§2–3, 13–14. Repo: briefing, radio, fieldwork, return, and debrief exist; deployment is compressed.)*

2. **One bounded discrepancy; no encounter obligation.** One pre-existing structural inconsistency exists in authoritative state. It is never spawned for pacing, need not be noticed, and need not escalate. No entity, chase, combat, or new phenomenon system is required. *(Basis: Doctrine Laws IX, XXIII–XXIV, LII–LIII; Reconstruction Authority §§11–13, 28. Repo: persistent geography exists; active phenomena remain incomplete and out of scope.)*

3. **A persistent human team, not a scripted cast.** The specimen has four participants and the player leads. Coworkers retain identity, knowledge, condition, equipment, task, memory, and consequences; they may disagree or act in parallel. Names are not invariant, and four is specimen scope—not a global staffing rule. *(Basis: Gameplay Constitution §§6–7, 17; Reconstruction Authority §§9–10, 24. Repo: seeded staffing and task/order state exist; deep, reload-safe humanity does not.)*

4. **Deterministic world, generative performance.** Canonical authorities resolve and persist geography, outcomes, observations, evidence, delivery, personnel effects, reports, and institutional updates. A provider may interpret bounded intent and phrase observer-safe output; it may not create facts. Offline fallback must complete the operation. *(Basis: Doctrine §§0.2–0.4; Gameplay Constitution §§4–5; Reconstruction Authority §4. Repo: core authorities and fallback exist; persistence/provider repair is incomplete.)*

5. **Natural agency without player theft or machinery friction.** The player may investigate, measure, photograph, note, flag, continue, ignore, retreat, or delegate while acting. Ambiguity prompts clarification; constraints produce legible partial failure. The system never invents player speech, emotion, action, or decision. *(Basis: Doctrine §§2.3–2.4, 7.4–7.7, 7.29; Gameplay Constitution §§5–7, 10. Repo: simple actions are safe; compound agency and human LOCAL response are gaps.)*

6. **Evidence is the bridge, not a collectible.** The chain is **observation → measurement/photo/note/testimony → persistent evidence object → report claim → institutional interpretation**. Provenance, observer, time/place, equipment, custody, access, and uncertainty persist. Evidence may be partial, lost, contradicted, or undelivered. *(Basis: Doctrine Chapters IV, XI; Gameplay Constitution §12; Reconstruction Authority §§5, 16–17. Repo: capture, custody, archive, reporting, contradiction, and Standard access exist; analysis is partial.)*

7. **Truth, belief, and consequence remain separate.** What happened, what observers sensed, what evidence survived, what the player reported, and what A-Sync believes never overwrite one another. Player choice must visibly affect both human/operational outcome and institutional belief. *(Basis: Doctrine Chapters III–V; Reconstruction Authority §§5–8, 17. Repo: claim/truth separation and abort/return/debrief persist; institutional cognition remains partial and split.)*

8. **Comparable runs with controlled variance.** Mission structure and discrepancy stay fixed. Seeded variance is limited to workplace texture: prior reviewer, one equipment annoyance, coworker state, one uncertain prior fact, or who notices first. It is authoritative and persisted, never improvised by prose. *(Basis: deterministic randomness/persistence doctrine and the locked conversational decision. Repo: seeded staffing/geography exist; this package does not.)*

**Acceptance boundary.** Score every run for **agency, knowledge, continuity, tone, institutional, and human breaks**; track **friction breaks** internally. Pass only when the full UI route proves meaningful choice, evidence-to-belief causality, persistent human/world consequences, save/reload equivalence, and provider-independent completion. It does not prove campaign breadth, entities, deep separation/stress behavior, or long-horizon institutional evolution.

**Reconciled stale assumptions.** “One discrepancy” is a specimen condition, not an engagement director. “Persistent four-person team” does not require Jack/Nora/Alex/Steven or permanent global staffing. Deployment ritual is required despite current compression. V03 persistence changes remain uncommitted work, not a certified foundation.
