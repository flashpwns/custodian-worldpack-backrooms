# Project Reference Expedition — Specimen Brief

**Status:** Owner-ratified governing milestone proof; not a claim of current completion.

**Depends on:** *Project Reference Expedition — Invariants*.

**Authority:** Subordinate to `SIMULATION_DOCTRINE.md`, the Gameplay Constitution, admitted canon/source policy, `YELLOW_BEAST_RECONSTRUCTION_AUTHORITY.md`, and the Invariants.

**Implementation authority:** None beyond the current approved pass. The current repository does not yet satisfy this specimen.

**Purpose:** define the bounded playable proof capable of demonstrating the Yellow Beast player promise and exposing the current architecture’s real strengths and failures.

## 1. The claim this specimen must prove

Yellow Beast can make a routine A-Sync field assignment compelling through credible work, persistent people, natural agency, bounded uncertainty, and consequences—without requiring an entity encounter, scripted scare, or language model to decide reality.

The proof is one complete, repeatable operation from briefing through debrief. It is both a vertical slice and a controlled laboratory specimen. A successful run should leave the player thinking less about systems and more about what the team saw, what they chose, what evidence survived, and whether A-Sync now believes the right thing.

## 2. Fixed expedition frame

- **Operation:** preliminary layout and condition survey of a modest frontier section reached through known Complex-side infrastructure.
- **Team:** four people total—the player as field leader plus three persistent coworkers assigned survey, documentation, and route/safety responsibilities. Names may be seeded; roles and identities must remain stable through the operation and reload.
- **Assignment:** verify the known approach, measure and document the frontier section, update route markers, preserve useful evidence, maintain accountability, and return.
- **Core condition:** one authoritative structural discrepancy exists before deployment. It is not created in response to pacing or player attention.
- **Threat envelope:** no entity, chase, combat, or mandatory injury. Ordinary environmental or equipment friction may occur only when causally established and legible.
- **Completion envelope:** successful completion, incomplete return, or controlled abort are all legitimate. Death and campaign-scale progression are outside this specimen.
- **Runtime:** the entire operation must be completable offline. Provider enrichment may improve language but may not change state or available outcomes.
- **Target duration:** approximately 35–60 minutes for a first deliberate playthrough. Comparative runs may become shorter as the player learns the work.

## 3. The structural discrepancy

The reference discrepancy is a **locally ordinary passage whose measured depth overlaps a previously surveyed parallel corridor**. Every immediate surface appears mundane. The contradiction emerges only when current measurements, known route geometry, and the prior survey are compared.

The discrepancy must obey these rules:

- It exists as world state whether or not the player notices it.
- Local observations remain concrete: dimensions, wall material, fixtures, route, sound, and recorded measurements.
- No interface labels it an anomaly or supplies a cause.
- A photograph alone cannot prove the spatial contradiction; measurement and record comparison provide stronger support.
- Coworkers may notice different pieces based on role, attention, position, and knowledge.
- Rechecking may confirm the measurements without explaining them.
- Continuing, marking, reporting, ignoring, or retreating cannot reveal an authored “correct answer.”
- The discrepancy remains persistent after the operation. A-Sync’s record of it may remain absent, uncertain, disputed, or provisionally accepted.

This is a specimen constraint, not a universal Yellow Beast mission template. Its purpose is to test whether trusted routine can make one impossible relationship matter.

## 4. Player experience arc

### A. Briefing

The player receives a boring, legible work order: route, survey boundary, known prior information, reporting expectations, issued equipment, team roster, and return conditions. Nothing in the briefing advertises horror. One item of prior information may be uncertain, but its uncertainty and source must be explicit.

### B. Staging and deployment

The player sees who is going, who holds which equipment, and whether the loadout can perform the assignment. At least one meaningful preparation decision is required: accept the issued loadout, request or reassign one relevant item, or knowingly depart with a limitation. The team performs accountability, radio procedure, and a perceptible Threshold crossing. These actions establish A-Sync and the route home as credible support systems.

### C. Established-route work

The team follows familiar infrastructure into the survey area. Early work establishes normality through navigation, measurements, photographs, marker checks, equipment handling, concise LOCAL coordination, and STANDARD check-ins. Coworkers should display useful competence and recognizable behavior without constant chatter.

### D. Frontier survey

The player decides work order and delegation. Natural instructions may combine a coworker task with a simultaneous player attempt—for example, asking the documentation specialist to photograph a fixture while the player checks a wall measurement. Custodian resolves duration, equipment, access, hearing, and partial success; prose reports only legitimate results.

### E. Discrepancy recognition

The contradiction becomes discoverable through accumulated work, not an announcement. The player may notice it, be prompted by a coworker’s bounded observation, or miss it. Recognition creates questions, not certainty. Team reactions depend on knowledge, role, relationship, and operational state.

### F. Leadership decision

The player may remeasure, photograph, compare records, mark the location, ask for corroboration, contact STANDARD, continue the assignment, ignore the discrepancy, or initiate return/abort. STANDARD acts from delivered information and current institutional knowledge, not field omniscience. Coworkers may advise or object, but the player retains field responsibility.

### G. Return, reporting, and debrief

The team physically returns with whatever people, equipment, and evidence remain. The player chooses what to report. Debrief must distinguish objective events, observations, evidence, testimony, report claims, and institutional conclusions. The final surface shows what changed without revealing hidden truth: mission disposition, evidence custody, team consequences, A-Sync belief, route status, and any justified follow-up.

## 5. Evidence and institutional belief

The minimum supported chain is:

**observation → measurement / photograph / note / testimony → evidence record → delivered report → institutional review**

Every evidence record needs stable identity, creator/observer, method, time, location, equipment, environmental context, custody, access, reporting state, and uncertainty. Evidence does not become knowledge merely because it exists. A-Sync can consider only testimony or records that legitimately reach its boundary.

The same world truth must support materially different institutional outcomes:

- **Strong record:** corroborated measurements, photographs, returned custody, and a clear report can produce a provisional spatial-discrepancy record and route review.
- **Weak record:** testimony or an unsupported measurement can produce an unresolved field claim.
- **Undelivered record:** the team may know while STANDARD does not.
- **Omission:** the discrepancy remains true while the institutional model stays unchanged.
- **Contradiction:** evidence and prior survey may coexist as disputed records without forced resolution.

## 6. Human-system requirements

Each coworker needs a stable operational identity visible through action rather than adjective-heavy biography. For this specimen, each must have:

- a role and relevant competence;
- a starting operational tendency or mood;
- observer-bounded knowledge;
- assigned equipment and current task;
- a recognizable communication pattern;
- the ability to comply, delay, clarify, refuse, or recommend;
- memory of material expedition events and player leadership choices;
- a persistent post-operation condition and attitude delta where causally justified.

This is not a demand for unrestricted autonomous minds. It is the minimum required for the team to feel human and for leadership to have consequences.

## 7. Controlled variance

The mission, route envelope, discrepancy, and acceptance checks remain fixed. A deterministic seed may vary only a small workplace package:

- who reviewed the prior survey;
- one equipment annoyance or limitation;
- coworker starting state;
- one uncertain briefing fact;
- who first notices a relevant detail.

Variance must be canonical, persisted, and visible through legitimate state. The provider may phrase it but may not select or invent it. At least one no-variance reference seed must remain available for exact regression comparison.

## 8. Minimum interface

The specimen needs one coherent observer-safe presentation stream plus clear access to:

- ACTION;
- LOCAL and STANDARD;
- assignment and current responsibility;
- team/contact state;
- equipment and custody;
- known map/route information;
- evidence records;
- return readiness and blockers.

The player should never need internal IDs, rigid incantations, or unexplained error codes. Unsupported or ambiguous language should yield a natural clarification or a legible constraint. No elaborate archive, career, audio, or cinematic system is required beyond what makes this operation understandable and inhabitable.

## 9. Acceptance matrix

The specimen is not accepted until all of the following are demonstrated through the production UI:

1. A complete offline run from briefing through debrief.
2. Meaningful staging and a perceptible Threshold crossing.
3. At least one successful compound coworker-plus-player instruction.
4. Nonblank, contextually human LOCAL interaction with each coworker.
5. Discovery through work rather than an anomaly notification.
6. Evidence capture, custody, reporting, and institutional interpretation with provenance intact.
7. Three contrasting runs: investigate/document, report/return early, and ignore/continue or omit.
8. A legible difference in team outcome and A-Sync belief between those runs.
9. Exact save/reload continuity before, during, and after the operation, within the project’s sanctioned persistence boundaries.
10. Provider-off and provider-switch runs that preserve identical canonical reality.
11. Active scoring for agency, knowledge, continuity, tone, institutional, and human breaks, with friction breaks tracked internally.
12. Human playtest evidence that ordinary work remains understandable and worth doing before the discrepancy appears.

Any unexplained state rewrite, knowledge leak, invented player speech, blank successful dialogue, ceremonial deployment, evidence shortcut, or machine-facing interaction failure keeps the specimen open.

## 10. Repository posture and exclusions

The current Clear-Q4 repository already provides useful foundations: deterministic action resolution, bounded persistent geography, seeded staffing, equipment custody, communications state, evidence provenance/archive, mission deviation, controlled abort, physical return, debrief, and offline fallback.

The specimen still depends on targeted completion or repair of deployment ritual, compound agency, human LOCAL behavior, personnel continuity, the fixed discrepancy contract, evidence-to-belief processing, and certified canonical save/reload behavior. Current uncommitted V03 persistence work is evidence of progress, not an accepted dependency.

Explicitly excluded: entities, new phenomenon families, large procedural expansion, multiple mission types, campaign progression, deep facility simulation, personnel hiring, advanced inventory scarcity, full separation/stress simulation, elaborate audiovisual presentation, and work justified only by later-game possibility.

**Exit condition:** the Reference Expedition becomes the trusted playable standard against which every retained, simplified, frozen, or removed Yellow Beast subsystem can be judged.
