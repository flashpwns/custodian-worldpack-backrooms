# Roadmap

## YB-1: Canon Foundation and Live World-Pack Repository

Source/claim model, small curated registry, minimal Threshold Baseline, and
conformance tooling.

## Later, only after explicit source review

- Verified location and chronology intake.
- Additional controlled survey scenarios.
- Evidence and communication cases grounded in admitted claims.
- Carefully scoped environmental rules where primary support exists.

Not planned by default: creature behavior, Still Life mechanics, Null Zone
generation, temporal displacement, green-glow mechanics, procedural architecture,
movie character arcs, or speculative Standard/Complex exchange.
