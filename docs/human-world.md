# Canon-first human world

Pass 2 makes the small currently admitted human slice experiential without turning sparse source material into a speculative organization chart. The source-backed runtime paths are ASYNC identification, staffed Threshold operation, protected inspection, alert/recording behavior, and a recorded surveillance view. Each has a normalized claim, authority, source locator through that claim, and an appropriate Q4 or Beck delivery path.

Clear-Q4 encounters this material through facility staffing around the Threshold, bounded inspection and documentation practice, equipment, and chosen radio transmissions. Beck encounters it through bounded field records, alerts, and recorded material. A report is an institutional account, not objective truth; delivery and review remain explicit communication steps.

Roles are not people. Current generic field researchers, engineers, researchers, and other desk personnel are persistent procedural role occupants with explicit `pack-original-human-glue` provenance. They fill mundane operational gaps without acquiring a distinct biography. The normalized corpus still does not support admitting a named canon character, so no named cast was invented for this pass.

The generic operations desk, field-report form, and staffing assignment are likewise conservative glue. They remain visible to developer reports but never expose provenance, claims, or source metadata in ordinary play. Staff retain their identity and assignment state in a world; if an individually instantiated character dies, the canonical character-continuity rules remain irreversible and a role is filled only by a different identity.

Nullzone and Lost do not receive ASYNC terminology or institutional records from these additions. They can only encounter human objects, evidence, or people through legitimate shared-world observation and communication. Derived threads may later recognize personnel status, reports, and vacancies, but do not form a second personnel ledger or change a person's status.
