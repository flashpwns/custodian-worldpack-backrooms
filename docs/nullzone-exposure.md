# Nullzone Exposure

Nullzone Exposure is a civilian investigation loop over `yellow-beast-world-history@v1`: prepare at a persistent personal base, enter the declared scenario-specific access point, revisit or expand a persistent generated region, recover an object, then return and archive it.

The base, archive, routes, and civilian hypotheses are world-history materialization, not a second world database. Civilian hypotheses are explicitly non-authoritative. The safe civilian projection excludes ASYNC management, institutional records, hidden attention, undiscovered topology, and unseen artifacts.

The first-excursions fixture uses a pack-original repeatable access relationship, one unidentified-object artifact class, a personal archive, bounded route expansion, and a deterministic failed-return path that leaves a recoverable remnant. A detectable trace can create a limited institutional record, but the civilian neither receives ASYNC records nor sees the internal attention stage.
# Nullzone investigative experience

Nullzone is a repeating personal-evidence loop: home, review, preparation, excursion, return, and comparison. Archive entries retain personal recovery provenance; notebook questions and hypotheses remain personal interpretation rather than world truth. Current observation wins over remembered routes or old evidence. The experience works offline and never supplies ASYNC knowledge unless legitimately learned.
