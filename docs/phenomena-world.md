# Phenomena and entities

Yellow Beast admits anomalous content through the normal source → locator → claim → authority → runtime trace. A phenomenon is an observed condition or event. It is not promoted to an entity merely because its cause is unknown. The current admitted FF2 items are source-local, deterministic scenario/history admissions: a marked surface discontinuity and a later local transition observation. Neither gains an inferred cause, recurrence rule, or hazard.

An entity is an individuated presence only when the governing authority supports that interpretation. Capability records are restrictive: unsupported movement, perception, communication, harm, motive, and reaction are absent rather than probable. A unique observation does not become a population or spawn table. The older FF2/YB-23 Still Life fixture remains stationary because that record admits no further behavior; it is not the current Clear-Q4 Still Life family authority.

Pass 16B adds the authoritative Clear-Q4 ecology in `tools/q4-phenomenon-ecology.js`. It preserves exact canonical families while projecting only observer-established designations. Its Still Life instances have stable, bounded, heterogeneous profiles derived once and persisted. Its Bacteria instances use acquired speech, legal spatial pursuit, canonical capture, and environment-contact consequences. Neither family is a generic spawn loop or combat system. Production eligibility applies only to newly generated persistent geography and is deliberately biased toward no phenomenon.

Phenomenon effects use canonical world history. Observers can perceive an effect, capture it as evidence, or report it; these are separate from the objective event. Beck receives only explicit report content. Q4, Nullzone, and Lost see observer-safe descriptions, not internal terms, causes, or source metadata. Provider narration receives only those safe facts and is rejected if it invents anomalous behavior or player interiority.

Generic Backrooms taxonomies, hostile-creature mechanics, random scare rolls, and fandom labels are not admission paths. Quiet turns remain quiet. Derived work may consume an unresolved condition only after legitimate reporting makes it institutional knowledge; similar observations never become a common cause, entity population, or new capability.
