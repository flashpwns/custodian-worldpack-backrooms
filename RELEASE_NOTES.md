# Yellow Beast 0.2.0-alpha.1

## What works

Async: Clear-Q4 is playable with LOOK, MOVE, INSPECT, USE, save/resume, structured commands, and the no-network Offline Interpreter. Optional OpenAI-powered natural-language interpretation and narration use your own `OPENAI_API_KEY`; the tested default is `gpt-5.6-luna`, overridable with `YELLOW_BEAST_AI_MODEL`. Provider use may incur charges and always falls back safely to offline play.

macOS and Windows portable artifacts bundle Node and Custodian. They are unsigned alpha builds. Only Async: Clear-Q4 is fully playable; other modes remain experimental. This release has no final GUI or installer, a deliberately small gameplay scope, and alpha save compatibility may evolve.
